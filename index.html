Interactive Field Deployment Guide

How to Deploy PickleVAR Pro on Any Pickleball Court

Designed for zero court Wi-Fi: use one phone's Personal Hotspot, mount a minimum of 2 Camera Node phones on tripods at opposite corners, and scan the Session QR Code.

1

Turn On Phone Wi-Fi Hotspot

Enable Personal Hotspot on the Controller phone/tablet at the net post. Connect all Camera Node phones to this hotspot for sub-15ms peer-to-peer WebRTC transport.

Works on outdoor courts without router
2

Diagonal Tripod Setup (Min 2 Nodes)

Mount CAM-1 at NW Corner and CAM-2 at SE Corner (1.5m–1.8m tripod height, 2.2m behind baseline, tilted 25° down). Two diagonal cameras see all 4 baselines, sidelines, and Kitchen lines.

Scales up to 4+ nodes via QR
3

Scan Controller QR Code

Tap Pair Camera Phone on the Controller to display the Session QR Code. Scan it from each Camera Node phone to automatically open Camera Node mode and lock the WebRTC stream.

Auto-configures `?session=ID&role=node`
4

One-Tap Auto-Calibrate & Play

Tap Auto-Calibrate All on the Controller. Each phone snaps its 4 homography pins to the court lines and calls "IN!" or "OUT!" with audio + strobe alerts on impact.
