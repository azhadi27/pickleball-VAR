Visual Field Deployment Guide

How to Setup PickleVAR Camera Nodes & Controller

Step 1 • Court Corner Placement Min 2 Nodes (Diagonal)

Mount CAM-1 (NW) and CAM-2 (SE) on tripods 4 to 6 ft outside opposite diagonal corners so every baseline and sideline is visible. Add CAM-3 & CAM-4 for full quad-corner redundancy.

Step 2 • Tripod Height & Tilt Angle 1.5m – 2.2m Elevation

Elevate the tripod to 5–7 ft (1.5m–2.2m) and tilt the phone downward 20°–30°. Higher elevation prevents players' bodies from occluding the far boundary lines.

Step 3 • QR Pairing & 4-Corner Calibration Auto-Hough Line Snap

Scan the Controller's QR Code on each tripod phone. Tap Auto-Detect 4 Corners (or drag the `TL`, `TR`, `BR`, `BL` pins) to lock the 2D inverse homography matrix.

Step 4 • Parabolic Bounce Apex Verification Sub-Frame Line Call

As the ball travels across frames, PickleVAR tracks vertical velocity reversal ($\Delta v_y$) to pinpoint the exact ground bounce contact footprint and triggers instant audio/visual IN or OUT calls.
